★ Nominated · Digital Transformation Award

Accredible Enterprise Migration & Governance Transformation

University of Pittsburgh · Pitt EDGE · 2026. A comprehensive transformation of Pitt EDGE's Accredible digital credential ecosystem — moving from a centralized credentialing model to an enterprise-wide governance framework that distributed ownership across academic and administrative units while improving security, scalability, and operational sustainability. Led by a small team, nominated for a Digital Transformation Award for this work.

The Challenge

The platform supported 33,121 issued credentials, 15,583 recipients, and 759 credential groups. Growing adoption had outpaced the original governance model, creating permissions, ownership, and scalability challenges that needed a more sustainable operational structure.

My Role

Led governance review activities, user-access audits, migration tracking, stakeholder coordination, validation workflows, and quality assurance for the migration initiative — implementing a governance redesign, building Airtable and Excel migration-tracking processes, supporting the reorganization of credential groups, and helping establish a distributed ownership model across eleven institutional units.

Results

Administrative accounts dropped from 22 to 9 — a 59% reduction in privileged access — while total accounts decreased by 24%, all without losing operational coverage.
